Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026
SLEEP IN respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SLEEP IN app.

1. Information We Collect

SLEEP IN does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with us or with third-party advertising or analytics services.

SLEEP IN does not require you to create an account or sign in.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

SLEEP IN stores certain information locally on your device in order to provide app features, including:

  • Wake-up Call settings
  • Current stay information
  • Stay history
  • App preferences and settings

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

SLEEP IN offers in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple’s App Store.

Purchase and subscription transactions are processed by Apple using StoreKit. SLEEP IN does not receive or store your payment card information.

Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

4. Wake-up Call and Alarms

SLEEP IN uses Apple's AlarmKit framework to provide the optional Wake-up Call feature.

Alarm settings are used only to schedule alarms requested by you. SLEEP IN does not use alarm permissions for advertising, tracking, or unrelated purposes.

5. Analytics and Tracking

SLEEP IN does not currently use:

  • Third-party analytics services
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Cross-app tracking
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location tracking
  • Third-party crash reporting services

SLEEP IN does not sell user data.

6. Stay History

Your stay history, including hotel name, room number, check-in time, and check-out time, is stored locally on your device.

SLEEP IN does not upload this information to an external server.

Removing the app may remove locally stored information, subject to iOS system behavior.

7. Third-Party Services

SLEEP IN uses Apple system services, including:

  • App Store and StoreKit for purchases and subscriptions
  • AlarmKit for Wake-up Calls

These services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

SLEEP IN does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because SLEEP IN does not collect personal information through its own servers, no child-specific personal information is stored by us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if SLEEP IN's features or data practices change.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
